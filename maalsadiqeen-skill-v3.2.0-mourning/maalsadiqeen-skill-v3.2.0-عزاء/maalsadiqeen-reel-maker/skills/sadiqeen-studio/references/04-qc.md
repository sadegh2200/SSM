# 4 · QC — the gate, the numbers, and the five minutes

A delivery is a **verdict**, not a file. Two halves: the machine checks the numbers, and you look
at the sheet with the numbers in your head.

## 4.1 The gate, in order

```bash
python3 brandgate.py spec.json                       # words — before any frame exists
python3 studio.py build spec.json --out deliver/     # gate → frames → mix → mux → verify
python3 studio.py verify deliver/reel.mp4 --spec spec.json --sheet deliver/contact-sheet.png
```

`brandgate` runs the installed skills' own checkers:

| checker | owner skill | fails on |
|---|---|---|
| `copy_lint.py` | maalsadiqeen-reel | «…» placeholders · forbidden wording · dialect · emoji · Latin · >1 `<em>` per line · sacred text without tashkeel · seal without a grade · no CTA/brand card · >1 hero · word budget · non-contiguous scenes · >60 s · a verse with no recitation · no «الأثر في الحياة» · a Ma‛ṣūm named without the honourific |
| `ar_copycheck.py --spec` | arabic-editor | hamza · ta-marbuta · ASCII digits · «!» · « ؟» · three-dot ellipsis · dialect · names without honourifics · over-long sentences · tatweel · tashkeel density |
| `sanad_check.py --spec` | hadith-sanad | an invented seal · a card marked `needs_verification` · a quote with no citation |
| `hook_score_ar.py` | maalsadiqeen-reel | advisory: < 65/100 is a rewrite, not a build |

`build` refuses on a fail (override: `--force`, never for a delivery). `verify` reruns all of them
and appends them to `report.md`, so the report you hand over contains the copy gate too.

## 4.2 The numbers `verify` measures

| check | wanted | failing means |
|---|---|---|
| frame | 1080×1920 | the crop is wrong |
| fps | 30.000 | a variable-rate export slipped in |
| duration | spec ±0.12 s | a block was dropped or a fade overran |
| video codec | h264 · yuv420p | the platform will re-encode |
| audio | aac | silent master shipped as a delivery |
| loudness | −15.5 ±1.2 LUFS | it will sound weak next to another reel |
| true peak | ≤ −1.0 dBTP | clipping on a phone speaker |
| body contrast | ≥ 6:1 | unreadable in sunlight |
| palette | no pure black / pure white | a different account's look |
| red-free | no blood red | off-brand, especially in mourning |
| word budget | ≤ hard = `max(110, 2.9·duration)` | nobody is reading, they are scrolling |
| hero count | exactly 1 | no screenshot frame, or two |

## 4.3 The five minutes with your eyes

In this order, on the contact sheet first, then on the video at 1×:

1. **0.0–1.5 s** — does the hook land before the thumb moves? One idea, one line, no preamble.
2. **the hero** — is this the frame someone would screenshot? If not, the hero is the wrong line.
3. **the proof** — can you read book, volume, page without pausing twice?
4. **the joins** — no clipped word at the safe-zone edges, no orphan, nothing under the watermark.
5. **the last frame** — the card alone, uncluttered, 1.5 s of silence.
6. **the mute test** — watch it with the sound off: everything still makes sense?
7. **the phone test** — look at it at 30 % size: the hierarchy still reads?

## 4.4 Symptom → cause → fix

| you see | it is | fix |
|---|---|---|
| a word drifts away from its neighbour | advances rounded per glyph | advance in supersampled space (`typo._rasterise`) |
| glyphs look 10 % small, joins gappy | `set_char_size` (72/64 dpi) | `set_pixel_sizes` |
| a box (▯) inside a line | the face has no glyph | `_pieces()` fallback; draw `﴿ ﴾` |
| a block sits 100 px too high | centred on the font box | centre on `Ink.img.height` / baseline grid |
| a «—» appears above the first word | runs painted in logical order | reverse runs before painting |
| paragraphs look amateur | centred | `paragraph(..., align="right")` |
| the cream palette has an orange cast | light sweep at charcoal strength | `k = 0.28` for `main` |
| the proof card's last line runs under the watermark | block packed from its top | pack from `BOTTOM = 1322` upward |
| the reel feels rushed | a reveal < 0.9 s from the cut | extend the scene |
| the reel feels empty | one dense block missing | add `paragraph`/`qa`, not more short blocks |

## 4.5 When not to deliver

Do not hand over a reel that: fails a gate; has an unverified sacred line; has a proof block with
no reference; reads as two ideas; is louder or quieter than ±1.2 LUFS; or whose Persian summary
cannot say what is uncertain. Say what is missing and what you need — one message, three questions
at most.
