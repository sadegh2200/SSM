# Brand voice — «مع الصادقين»

> نُعيد النصّ إلى موضعه: مع النصّ، لا مع الظنّ.

This is the identity the scripts enforce. `config/brand.json` holds the machine-readable half
(CTA lists, hashtags, limits, forbidden wording); this file holds the judgement.

## 1 · The promise

**One text, its source, and what it asks of you this week — in forty calm seconds.**

Everything else is optional. If a reel does not keep that promise it is not on-brand, however well
it performs. The page is not a news account, not a debate club, not a quote wall.

## 2 · Four traits

| trait | we do | we never |
|---|---|---|
| **عِلميّ** (scholarly) | name the book, the volume, the page; say when we do not know | imply a grading, round a reference, "some scholars say" |
| **هادئ** (calm) | state, explain, leave the judgement to the viewer | raise the voice, shame, threaten, use «!!» |
| **واضح** (clear) | one idea, one hinge word, one call to action | pile up synonyms, hide the point in the caption |
| **مُتَّسِع** (generous) | speak to every Muslim reader; quote what unites | attack a group, name opponents, reply in kind |

The order matters. Calm without knowledge is empty; knowledge without calm is a fight.

## 3 · Register

- **Classical Arabic (فصحى)** everywhere on screen. No dialect, no Egyptian/Levantine/Iraqi forms,
  no transliteration.
- **Fully vocalised** (تشكيل) on sacred text and on the hinge word; modern standard elsewhere may be
  light on tashkeel.
- Short sentences. One clause per line where the screen allows it.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no question-exclamation («؟!»), no ALL-CAPS equivalents.
- Address the viewer as **أنت** rarely and deliberately; **نحن** when the point is shared.
- Latin script only for `@maalsadiqeen`.

### Lexicon

| prefer | avoid |
|---|---|
| النصّ، الآية، الرواية، السند، الدرجة | المعلومة، الكلام، الحكاية |
| المعنى، المفهوم، الدلالة | السرّ، الحقيقة المخفية |
| نقرأ، نفهم، نتأمّل | نكشف، نصدم، نحذّر |
| ينبغي، الأولى، الأوفق | يجب عليك، إلّا إذا، وإلّا |
| قال الله، قال الإمام، ذكر العلّامة | يقولون، يُقال، في بعض المصادر |

## 4 · The three layers — the core distinction

A viewer must be able to tell, **from the screen alone**, which layer a sentence belongs to.

| layer | what it is | how it is marked | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **نصّ** | the verse or the ḥadīth, verbatim | ﴿ ﴾ for Qurʾān; « » + book/volume/page for ḥadīth | ﴿وَكُونُوا مَعَ الصَّادِقِينَ﴾ |
| **شرح** | a named scholar's explanation | the scholar's name in the attribution line | «هُمُ الَّذِينَ صَدَّقُوا اللهَ» — الإمام الرضا (ع)، الكافي ج١ ص٢٠٨ |
| **استنباط** | our own inference | «وَمِنْ ذَلِكَ…» / «نستفيد…» / a line that is visibly not a quote | ومن ذلك أنّ المعية اتّباعٌ لا مجرّد مصاحبة |

Rules:

- Never present an inference in the typography of a verse (﴿ ﴾) or inside « ».
- Never present a scholar's opinion as the text itself; the name is part of the sentence.
- Never present the text as our opinion — no "أرى أنّ الآية تعني" when the āyah is clear.
- In the caption, the same order: نصّ → شرح → استنباط → أثر. A reader should be able to stop after
  any layer and still have something true.

## 5 · Source etiquette

- A reference is `الكتاب جX صY` (Arabic-Indic digits). A grading is quoted only if stated.
- **No source → no proof block.** Say it in the summary, keep the reel about what is known.
- Distinguish: `صحيح السند` (the chain is sound) ≠ `صحيح المتن` (the content is sound) ≠ `معتبر`.
  Do not upgrade one to another.
- If a reference is disputed, say «وقد نوقش سنده» — do not hide it and do not build a scandal on it.
- Saying **«لا أعلم»** is on-brand and costs nothing. Guessing costs the page.
- When quoting a scholar, quote the printed edition; a website is a lead, not a citation.

## 6 · «الأثر في الحياة» — mandatory

Every reel ends with one concrete change in the viewer's week. Not a slogan, not a feeling.

| good | why | bad | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| «لا تنسب قولاً إلى الله قبل أن تعرف سنده.» | a deed, checkable | «غيّر حياتك إلى الأفضل.» | slogan |
| «اسأل عن السند قبل أن تُرسل الرسالة.» | specific, social | «كن إيجابياً.» | empty |
| «إذا سمعت حديثاً في مجلس، اطلب اسم الكتاب.» | a situation | «تأمّل في عظمة الدين.» | no action |

Constraints: 4–32 words, one sentence, no fear, no promise of reward, no «ستتغيّر حياتك».
The builder can put it on screen for 2–4 s; otherwise it goes in the caption and comment slot 4.

## 7 · Speaking to everyone

The audience is Shia, but the page is not sectarian in tone.

- Quote what the text says; do not add «مخالفونا يقولون» or name a sect.
- If the material is a refutation, state the objection **fairly** in one line, then answer from the
  text. The viewer should feel the objection was understood, not defeated.
- Never insult a symbol, a scholar or a group. Never use «الشبهة» as a weapon; it means "a question
  worth answering".
- When the material is shared with Sunni readers, lead with the part that is common ground.

## 8 · Naming and honorifics

| who | on screen | in a tight line |
|---|---|---|
| الله | الله (no pronoun games) | — |
| النبي محمد | النبيّ صلّى الله عليه وآله | النبيّ (ص) |
| الأئمة | الإمام علي عليه السلام · الإمام الصادق عليه السلام | الإمام الصادق (ع) |
| فاطمة الزهراء | عليها السلام | (ع) |
| scholar | العلّامة المجلسي · الشيخ المفيد (no honourific required, no «قدّس سرّه» unless quoted) | — |

The honourific is never dropped for a Maʿṣūm. If the line does not fit, cut another word.

## 9 · The ending

A reel ends the way a good lesson ends: the point, one thing to do, and silence. The ending is not the
place to ask for a favour.

**The doctrine:** at most **one** invitation, and only if the text earned it. Never «أرسلها لمن تحب» —
that line asks the reader to feel something about us; the page asks the reader to understand something
about the text. The old formulas are *forbidden* now, not merely discouraged:

> أرسلها لمن تحب · أرسلها لمن يحتاجها · شاركها مع من تحب · انشرها

| the text is… | the ending is | example |
|---|---|---|
| a definition, a verse, a reference worth keeping | **save** | احْفَظْهَا لِلْعَوْدَةِ |
| an answer to a question people actually ask | **send to the asker** | أَرْسِلْهَا لِمَنْ سَأَلَكَ عَنْهَا |
| an explanation someone is already searching for | **send to the seeker** | أَرْسِلْهَا لِمَنْ يَبْحَثُ عَنْ هَذَا المَعْنَى |
| an occasion, a life, an objection, a ruling | **nothing** — the verse and the card | — |

The invitation is chosen **after** the copy, never before: if you cannot say who would need this exact
reel, it has no invitation. The icon follows the verb — a paper plane for sending, a bookmark for
saving, and no icon at all when there is no invitation.

The order of the last scene:

1. the take-away in one line (our inference, kufi);
2. the invitation — one line, 4–7 words, no exclamation, no promise;
3. the closing line — an āyah, a saying, or a three-word verdict;
4. the brand card, alone, ≥ 1.5 s.

A heavy text (a death, a ruling, an objection, Muharram) stops after (3). Selling a mourning reel is the
fastest way to lose the reader you spent forty seconds earning.

Forbidden: تابعونا · لايك · اشتركوا · شاركوا بقوة · لا تنسوا المتابعة · any request to follow, and any
sentence whose subject is *us* rather than the text.

## 10 · Forbidden (the brand fails here)

1. Fear-selling: death, hell, «مصيرك», «ستندم», threats in the second person.
2. Clickbait: «لن تصدّق», «السر», «صادم», «عاجل», «الحقيقة التي يخفونها».
3. Polemic: naming sects, insulting anyone, «الردّ على…» as a hook.
4. Invented authority: a seal without a grading, a reference without a page, «قال العلماء» with no name.
5. Dialect, slang, emoji, exclamation marks, Latin script (except the handle).
6. Promises: «ستُقضى حاجتك», «من قرأها…» unless the text itself says it and the source is given.
7. Political commentary, current events as a hook, mocking a public figure.
8. Cover-up: hiding a weak chain to keep a beautiful story.

## 11 · Self-check before the gate

1. Can a viewer tell which layer each line belongs to without the caption?
2. Is every sacred sentence exactly as in the source, fully vocalised?
3. Is every reference a real book, volume and page — or absent?
4. Is there a grading anywhere? If yes, is it stated by a named source?
5. Does the reel make one point, or two?
6. Is the «الأثر في الحياة» a deed someone could do tomorrow?
7. Could this reel be attacked for fear, bait or sectarianism? Fix it now.
8. Does the CTA come from the approved list?
9. Would R1 (the serious learner) save it, and R2 (the seeker) understand it?
10. Would you sign your name under every line?
